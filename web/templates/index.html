How the sizing numbers are calculated

Bill diff = Before-battery bill - Post-battery bill

Oracle perfect anual saving = Bill diff / DaysInCSV * 365

Realistic anual saving = max(0, Oracle perfect anual saving) * Real-world savings factor

Annual saving used below = Realistic anual saving

Battery investment = E battery * Battery cell price + P battery * Inverter/PCS price

Yearly maintenance = Battery investment * Yearly maintenance %

Annual net cashflow = Annual saving - Yearly maintenance

NPV = -Battery investment + sum(year = 1..ProjectYears, Annual net cashflow / (1 + DiscountRate)^year)

Payback = Battery investment / Annual net cashflow
If Annual net cashflow is zero or negative, payback shows Never.

Contract cap recommendation = Oracle post-battery highest monthly peak * 1.05
That 5% is a small buffer above the oracle peak, because real controllers are not magical future-seeing robots.

Pareto frontier? = whether this candidate is non-dominated among the selected custom battery options.

Oracle LP math

This is the optimization problem the oracle will solve later. The machine knows the real CSV load/PV data and chooses the unknown battery actions that minimize the bill.

Known parameters

Load(t)
Building load from the CSV, in kW.
PV(t)
Solar generation from the CSV, in kW.
BatteryCapacity
Battery energy capacity, in kWh.
BatteryPowerLimit
Maximum battery charge/discharge power, in kW.
ChargeEfficiency
Fraction of charging energy that becomes stored energy.
DischargeEfficiency
Fraction of stored energy that comes back out usefully.
dt
Time step in hours, detected automatically from the selected CSV. Current dt is {{ dt }} h.
EnergyPrice(t)
Electricity tariff at time t, in VND/kWh.
DemandChargeRate
Capacity charge, in VND/kW.
BatteryWearCost
Battery degradation cost, in VND/kWh throughput.
MinimumSOC
Lowest allowed battery state of charge.
MaximumSOC
Highest allowed battery state of charge.
RequiredFinalSOC
Minimum SOC required at the end of the horizon.

Unknown variables to solve

BatteryDischarge(t)
Battery to load power, in kW.
GridCharge(t)
Grid to battery power, in kW.
SolarCharge(t)
PV surplus to battery power, in kW.
GridImport(t)
Power imported from the grid, in kW.
BatterySOC(t)
Battery state of charge from 0 to 1.
PeakGrid
Monthly billed peak demand, in kW.

Objective

MINIMIZE:

  SUM over every time step t in the 30-day billing block (
      EnergyPrice(t)  dt  (
          GridCharge(t) - BatteryDischarge(t)
      )
  )
       Net energy cost

+ SUM over every time step t in the 30-day billing block (
      BatteryWearCost  dt  (
          BatteryDischarge(t)
        + GridCharge(t)
        + SolarCharge(t)
      )
  )
       Battery getting old

+ DemandChargeRate  PeakGrid
       Monthly demand charge

Constraints

GridImport(t)
  = EffectiveLoad(t) + GridCharge(t) - BatteryDischarge(t)
   0
       No electricity export

BatterySOC(t+1)
  = BatterySOC(t)
  + (ChargeEfficiency  dt / BatteryCapacity)  GridCharge(t)
  + (ChargeEfficiency  dt / BatteryCapacity)  SolarCharge(t)
  - (dt / (DischargeEfficiency  BatteryCapacity))  BatteryDischarge(t)
       Battery SOC update

0  BatteryDischarge(t)  BatteryPowerLimit
0  GridCharge(t)  BatteryPowerLimit
0  SolarCharge(t)  BatteryPowerLimit
0  SolarCharge(t)  SolarSurplus(t)

MinimumSOC  BatterySOC(t)  MaximumSOC
BatterySOC(FinalMonthStep) >= RequiredFinalSOC

SUM over k in Window(t) of Weight(t,k) * GridImport(k) <= PeakGrid
Window(t) covers exactly 0.5 hours; weights come from covered duration.
The number of steps is detected from the CSV and dt, never fixed at 96.
       Dynamic-resolution 30-minute demand
